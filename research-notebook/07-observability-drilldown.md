# Observability Drilldown

## Goal

Trace a suspicious observation down to the system behavior that caused it.

## Checklist

- identify the source process
- confirm exact timestamps
- review environment and permissions
- check related service or config changes
- compare against the baseline state
- verify whether the issue is transient or recurring

## Output format

- the symptom
- the likely cause
- what evidence confirms it
- what evidence contradicts it
- what should be checked next

## Why it matters

Observability is usually the difference between a random warning and a real operational issue. This drilldown turns raw data into actionable signal.

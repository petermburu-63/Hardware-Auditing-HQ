# Correlation Brief

## Objective

Tie together isolated observations into a single narrative so the pattern makes sense.

## Questions to answer

- Did the suspicious events occur around the same time?
- Were the affected assets or services connected?
- Was the same IP, process, or host involved in multiple events?
- Are the signs consistent with a broad drift pattern or a single isolated issue?

## Record

- event timestamps
- source devices or hosts
- active services
- user accounts and activities
- related log files or data sets

## Conclusion

Write the final conclusion as a concise operational summary that explains what likely happened, why it matters, and what to investigate next.

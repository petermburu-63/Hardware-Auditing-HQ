# Research Notebook

This section is meant to capture the deeper thinking behind the repo: what was observed, why it mattered, what was compared, and what should be hardened next.

## Purpose

The notebook exists to turn raw notes and technical observations into a research trail. It is designed for lab investigations, system review records, and evidence-based findings.

## Use

Use these files to document:

- baseline system state
- suspicious telemetry or behavior
- home-lab risk scenarios
- hardening actions and follow-up checks
- before-and-after comparisons
